    Interstate Cyber Crime Syndicate Profiler 🕵️

    Modus Operandi Dossier & Threat Actor Telemetry

    In-depth tactical profiling of organized fraud syndicates operating across Rajasthan, Mewat, and inter-state corridors.

    1. Mewat Vehicle & OLX Advance Syndicate

    Active Nexus
    Primary Corridor: Bharatpur, Alwar, Deeg & Border Districts · Estimated Reports: 120+

    Modus Operandi (MO): Impersonates Indian Army personnel posting second-hand vehicles or furniture on OLX/Facebook Marketplace. Claims army transfer and requests booking token via spoofed UPI QR codes that debit the victim rather than crediting.

    MITRE ATT&CK for Cybercrime:
    • T1566.002: Spearphishing Link / QR Code Weaponization
    • T1656: Impersonation of Defense Personnel
    • T1499: Fast-track Mule Bank Account Exfiltration

    2. JVVNL / AVVNL Bijli Disconnection Smishing Ring

    High Frequency
    Primary Corridor: Jaipur, Bikaner, Jodhpur, Kota · Estimated Reports: 94+

    Modus Operandi (MO): Sends bulk smishing messages at 7:00 PM: "Dear Consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill. Call electricity officer at 9123456789." Directs victims to install rogue APKs (`jvvnl_bill_pay.apk`) granting accessibility & SMS OTP forwarding.

    MITRE ATT&CK for Cybercrime:
    • T1204.002: Malicious APK File Execution
    • T1411: Accessibility Service & OTP Interception
    • T1078: Valid Credentials Siphonage

    3. Fake RTO e-Challan Phishing Nexus

    Critical Spread
    Primary Corridor: Jodhpur, Udaipur, Ajmer, Jaipur · Estimated Reports: 72+

    Modus Operandi (MO): Spoofs Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH) Parivahan portal. Sends SMS notifying of fake traffic challan with lookalike URL (`rto-challan-pay.xyz`) or rogue APK downloading banking trojans.

    MITRE ATT&CK for Cybercrime:
    • T1566.001: Smishing with Typosquatted Domain
    • T1189: Drive-by Compromise via Lookalike Gateway

    4. Digital Arrest & Fake CBI/ED Video Call Ring

    High Financial Impact
    Primary Corridor: Statewide Targeting Senior Citizens & Professionals · Avg Loss: Rs 8.5 Lakhs

    Modus Operandi (MO): Posing as CBI / ED / Police officers over WhatsApp/Skype video calls in mock police station setups. Threats of money laundering warrants followed by demands for funds transfer into "Supreme Court Verification" escrow accounts.

    MITRE ATT&CK for Cybercrime:
    • T1656: Deepfake & Uniform Impersonation
    • T1056: Coercive Social Engineering / Psychological Duress

    About Fraud Shield 🛡️
    
        
    Detect. Connect. Dismantle.
    
        
    Rajasthan Police Hackathon 2026 · Problem Statement 01: Cyber Fraud, Phishing & Malicious Apps
    
      
    

      
    
        
    ⚠️ Problem Statement (PS01)
    
        
    
          Cyber fraudsters operate organized smishing (SMS phishing), spoofed government portals (RTO e-Challan, JVVNL power bill), malicious QR refund traps, and rogue Android APK trojans across Rajasthan. Complaints arrive in isolated silos, preventing law enforcement from identifying the underlying synchronized scam syndicates.
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    ✅ The Fraud Shield Solution Architecture
    
        
    
          
    
            
    🔍
    
            
    1. Explainable AI & QR Engine
    
            
    
              RandomForest (0.6) + Explainable Rules (0.4). Analyzes TLD risk, brand lookalikes (Rapidfuzz), homoglyphs, UPI collect traps, and registration age. Supports English, Hindi & Hinglish.
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    📱
    
            
    2. APK Static Analyzer
    
            
    
              Inspects AndroidManifest permissions, flagging overlay attacks (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`), SMS OTP theft, and Accessibility abuse without ever executing untrusted binaries.
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    🔗
    
            
    3. NetworkX Clustering
    
            
    
              Connected component graph algorithms link separate complaints via shared IoCs (domains, phones, UPIs), reconstructing adversary networks into actionable campaign dossiers.
